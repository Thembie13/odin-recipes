Porridge Recipe(s)

Ingredients

  • water
  • milk
  • yellow cornmeal
  • ground cinnamon
  • sugar
  • peanut butter

Steps

  1. boil water in medium pot
  2. mix water and cornmeal in bowl, to make cornmeal wet
  3. add cornmeal in boiling water, and stir
  4. stop stirring when it starts bubbling, and partially cover pot
  5. after 10-15 minutes, add some milk to make more creamy
  6. add sweetners such as peanut butter and sugar, then stir
  7. continue to stir until it's done
